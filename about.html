Why the next great MGA will be built on AI.

Wholesale and excess & surplus property insurance still runs on email attachments, spreadsheets and phone calls. A broker sends a statement of values, an underwriter retypes it, and days pass before anyone sees a quote. Lattice Risk is a concept for a managing general agent that rebuilds that workflow around AI from the first line of code.

The problem

Submissions arrive in dozens of formats. Underwriters spend much of their time extracting data, checking appetite and chasing missing information rather than pricing risk. Good brokers get slow answers, and good risks get declined simply because nobody had time to look.

How it works

  • Intake: AI reads the submission, extracts locations, values and construction details, and flags what is missing.
  • Triage: The system scores each risk against carrier appetite and routes it in seconds.
  • Pricing support: Models combine catastrophe exposure, building characteristics and loss history into a recommended indication for the underwriter.
  • Human judgment: Licensed underwriters review and bind. AI does the busywork; people own the decisions.

Who benefits

Brokers get same-day indications instead of waiting days. Carriers get cleaner, better-documented risks and a faster path to growth in the E&S market. Insureds get coverage placed sooner and priced with more data behind it.

What comes next

The plan is to launch with commercial property, prove the speed and loss-ratio advantage with a small panel of carriers, then expand into adjacent E&S lines. Have a question? Ask the assistant in the corner of the page.
